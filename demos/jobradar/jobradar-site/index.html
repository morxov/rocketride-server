Why build another job tool

We reviewed LinkedIn, Indeed, Handshake, Simplify, Teal, Huntr, Jobright, Jobscan and others. They all cover search, alerts, a status board and a match score. These are the complaints none of them handle well.

The problemWhat JobRadar does
Ghost, stale and reposted listings: about 1 in 5 postings is a ghostEvery card shows when the role first appeared, how many times it was reposted, and whether it closed. Likely stale roles rank lower.
You hear about roles late, after the applicant count has grown a lotSources are re-checked every 10 minutes. A new match pops up as a notification and lands in New.
The same job, five times, from five boardsOne fingerprint per company and role merges duplicates into one card, linked to the company's own application page.
No follow-up reminders (Simplify, Teal and Huntr have none)Marking a job Applied schedules a follow-up a week later. The AI drafts the email; you send it.
Opaque match scores and AI that invents skillsEvery point has a reason you can read. Explain fit pairs each requirement with evidence from your own resume and lists honest gaps.
Paywalls, billing traps and auto-apply spamFree and local, with no auto-apply. You get a short "worth applying to today" list instead.

How it works

JobRadar is a RocketRide app: a React interface on the platform shell, driving four pipelines on a local RocketRide engine. Redis is its memory; Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite is the AI.

jobradar-tools.pipe

tools→tool_redis × 3tool_firecrawl

Every read and write the app makes. Each Redis node is locked to its own namespace: the job index, your data, and your seen and hidden marks.

profile.pipe

webhook→parse→prompt→llm_gemini

Turns a resume PDF into a structured profile: level, target roles, skills, locations and evidence.

writer.pipe

chat→prompt→llm_gemini→response

Explain fit, cover notes and follow-up emails. Told never to invent experience.

assistant.pipe

chat→agent_rocketride+llm_geminitool_redis (read)tool_redis (notes)tool_firecrawl

An agent that can read everything but write only to its own notes namespace.

What Redis does

First-seen index. A 32-shard index of every posting powers "new since your last check" and closed detection.

Marks that expire. Seen (45 days), hidden (90 days) and reminders with expiry, listed with SCAN.

A lock. SET NX with a 120-second expiry so two tabs never rebuild the index at once.

A cache. Job descriptions and fit explanations kept for 7 days, in front of Firecrawl and the LLM.

Counters. INCR for daily new-posting stats and applications per week.

Agent memory. A daily digest the assistant reads, and a namespace where it saves your reminders and notes.

Four-minute demo

Run on a local RocketRide engine with Redis in Docker.

  1. Profile and sources: drop a resume, or press Try it with a sample resume. The profile appears in seconds and the first check runs.
  2. Watch a company: add ramp or scaleai. Their postings pop up as new matches.
  3. Dashboard: show what is new since the last check, today's picks, and the stale count.
  4. Open a job and press Explain fit: requirements paired with resume evidence, and honest gaps.
  5. Save, then Mark applied. The tracker shows the follow-up reminder; Drafts writes the follow-up email.
  6. Assistant: "Which applications should I follow up on?", then "Remind me to prep for Ramp on Friday". The reminder appears in the tracker.
  7. To show the "new posting" moment again: Profile and sources → Replay the last 24 hours as new.
